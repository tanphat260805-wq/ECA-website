Corporate Standard

Quality Policy & Control Measures

At ECA ADVANCED FORGING SOLUTION, our Quality Policy centers on meeting or exceeding all product specifications and performance expectations, driving rigorous process controls to achieve Zero Defects before parts leave the floor, maintaining strict on-time delivery schedules for every shipment, and continuously upgrading our methods, technical training, and tooling technology. To uphold these standards, our Key Quality Control Measures ensure routine tooling maintenance and die repairs to prevent dimensional drift, deployment of high-accuracy optical measuring systems, digital gauges, and hardness testers, alongside systematic execution of Statistical Process Control (SPC) and Production Part Approval Processes (PPAP).

Quality Assurance Workflow

01

IQC (Incoming Quality Control)

Verification of raw coil strip dimensions, chemical composition, hardness, and surface integrity.

02

IPQC (In-Process Control)

Real-time optical dimension monitoring, burr height verification, and first-piece/last-piece approvals.

03

OQC (Outgoing Quality Control)

Full-lot sampling, final dimensional reports, clean degreasing check, and export-grade protective packaging.

Inspection & Measurement Equipment

High-precision metrology tools verifying critical dimensions and tight tolerances

VMM 4030 Video Measuring System

High-resolution optical magnification for complex part profiles, stamping edge quality, and micro-hole dimensions.

Werka Digital Caliper (600mm)

Heavy-duty large range digital caliper for stamping strip width, frame structure, and long-dimension verification.

INSIZE 1193-300 Digital Caliper

Range 0-300mm / 0.03mm accuracy for precision fast checking of part lengths, hole distances, and step heights.

Werka Height Gauge (300mm)

Precision dual-column height gauge mounted on granite base for precise stepped part thickness and offset verification.

Mitutoyo 293-240-30 Outside Micrometer

High-precision digital micrometer (0 - 25mm / 0.001mm resolution, IP65) for ultra-critical thickness and diameter checks.

Go / No-Go Pin Gauge Sets (3mm - 18mm)

Standardized cylindrical plug gauges ensuring zero-defect pass-through on precision holes, slots, and locators.

Precision Backed By Metrology

At ECA ADVANCED FORGING SOLUTION CO., LTD., precision is built on the synergy of rigorous process controls and advanced metrology. Guided by our philosophy of "Quality Without Compromise", we integrate high-precision measuring instruments—including VMM 4030 optical systems, Mitutoyo digital micrometers, INSIZE & Werka calipers and height gauges, and calibrated pin sets—directly into our 3-stage IQC – IPQC – OQC workflow. This end-to-end verification enables us to consistently eliminate manufacturing defects, safeguard tool integrity, and guarantee exact compliance with customer specifications down to ±0.005 mm tolerances across the Semiconductor, Automotive, Consumer Electronics, and Home Appliance sectors.
