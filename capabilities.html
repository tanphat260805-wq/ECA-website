Core Services

We focus on Design for Cost, Design for Quality, and Design for Manufacturing.

Tool & Die Design & Fabrication

ECA boasts a robust in-house team of experienced designers and complete tooling fabrication facilities. We are capable of designing and fabricating highly complex Progressive and Compound tools.

  • Tool Size Capability: Up to 2000 x 750 mm
  • Part Size Handled: From 10 x 10 mm to 450 x 1000 mm
  • Material Thickness: 0.15 mm to 4.0 mm
  • Specialties: Progressive tools, Compound tools

Precision Metal Stamping Production

We provide full-scale stamping production runs for critical components, supporting external metal stamping customers across multiple industries.

  • Progressive & Manual Stamping Operations
  • E&I and Stator Stack Laminations
  • High-precision Connector Pins fabrication
  • In-house tooling maintenance & modifications
  • Comprehensive engineering support from prototype to mass production

DTR Hob Cutter Supply

ECA is a trusted supplier of high-quality DTR Hob Cutters, ensuring extreme precision and durability for gear cutting operations.

  • DTR Standard Hob Cutters
  • DTR Involute Spline Hob Cutters
  • DTR Parallel Side Spline Hob Cutters
  • DTR Heavy Cutting Hobs
